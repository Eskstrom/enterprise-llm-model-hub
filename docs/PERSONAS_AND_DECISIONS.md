# Personas and Decisions

## AI platform administrator

Owns the approved LLM portfolio, spend controls, and production governance across teams.

**Decision:** Which models and capabilities are approved for which enterprise workloads?

## Product builder

Owns an AI feature and needs a safe, fast path from prototype to production.

**Decision:** Which model and routing policy meet the feature's quality, latency, and cost target?

## Security and procurement partner

Reviews provider exposure and verifies that platform choices satisfy organizational requirements.

**Decision:** Is the provider and model configuration acceptable for this customer, region, and data class?

## The key product moment

A product builder should be able to move from “I want to try a new model” to a traceable, approved production decision without having to reconstruct evaluation evidence, cost implications, or governance obligations manually.
